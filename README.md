Road Accident Data Analysis

* Analyzed 2 years of accident records to identify trends in accident frequency, severity, and contributing factors.
* Built interactive dashboards in Power BI to track KPIs such as total accidents, casualties by severity, and accident rate by vehicle type, road surface, and weather conditions.
* Performed time-series and geographical analysis to identify high-risk regions and accident hotspots.
* Segmented data by road type, lighting conditions, and vehicle category to uncover patterns for targeted safety measures.
* Provided data-driven recommendations for improving road safety and reducing accident rates.

*Tools: Power BI, DAX, Excel, Data Cleaning, Data Visualization, KPI Reporting
